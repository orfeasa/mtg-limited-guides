Build a deck, not a pile of bombs

  1. Start at 40 cards, about 17 lands. That leaves 23 spells. Adjust for your actual curve, fixing and mana sinks.
  2. Compare your best two-colour options. Count early board presence, useful answers, evasive threats and ways to keep finding cards. One rare does not choose your colours.
  3. Count the support. Artifacts for artifact payoffs; graveyard fuel for threshold; empower cards and defenders for Jace. Prepared spells and flashback cost mana later, too.
  4. Earn the splash. A powerful late single-pip card plus real fixing can justify it. Early double-pip spells rarely do. Count sources by when you need them, not just colour symbols.

Room of Refuge enters tapped and fixes one chosen colour. Heartwood makes only red or green. A three-mana fixer cannot fix your turn-two spell.

Four mechanics to remember

Empower Jace
Add loyalty to a Jace token; create one first only if you have none. −1: surveil 1. −3: draw a card. Normally activate one loyalty ability per planeswalker each turn, in your main phase with an empty stack. More loyalty does not grant another activation.
Prepare
Pay for the prepared spell and follow its timing. Casting it unprepares the permanent. If the permanent leaves first, the uncast copy disappears. A spell already cast remains on the stack independently. In the graveyard, the preparation card is a creature card, not an instant or sorcery for Stingcaster Mage.
Heartwood
An artifact token that taps for red or green and stays in play. Check whether its creator makes it enter tapped. Using it for mana does not sacrifice it.
Flashback & threshold
Flashback still obeys timing and additional costs, then exiles the spell when it leaves the stack. Threshold needs seven graveyard cards; spending a card from there can turn it off. Twinned Vision’s flashback also needs a discard.

Before trusting that combat

Read the opponent’s available colours and mana. These are reminders, not a complete trick list.

Academic Ascent
+2/+2 and flying, plus empower 2. A block can change dramatically.
Last Gasp
−3/−3. An undamaged 4/4 becomes 1/1; damage already marked still matters.
Wrath of the Bloodmane
Four damage to a creature or planeswalker. Costs one less with a legendary creature; an empower Jace token does not qualify.
Extended Absence
Instant exile for a creature or planeswalker. Exile does not cause a dies trigger.
Vigorbloom Charm
Can protect a permanent with hexproof and indestructible, or put a counter on a creature before it fights.
Silence the Echo , plus a cost
Sorcery: not a combat response. Budget a creature/planeswalker sacrifice or three extra mana. Without the sacrifice, it costs five mana total.

Budding Insurgent’s sacrifice ability is sorcery-speed. Do not plan it as a combat response. For an uncertain ruling, call the judge.

Between games: change something specific

  • What actually beat you? Speed, flyers, a bomb, an engine or mana trouble? Avoid rebuilding because of one unlucky draw.
  • Bring answers with real targets. Essence Burn needs a black or green creature or planeswalker. Narrow removal earns a slot when you have seen the target.
  • Against speed: trade expensive or slow value for early blockers and cheap interaction. Against a slow deck: consider resilient threats and repeatable value.
  • Make an explicit swap. Name the card out as well as the card in. Recount the deck and coloured sources after changes.

Last few minutes at home: do the prep situations, revisit missed interactions, then stop cramming. Bring sleeves, dice, tokens, a life pad, pen and a charged phone.
