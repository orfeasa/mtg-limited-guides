## What this supports

- Bloombrute: a promising life-gain payoff. At least five distinct draws are explicitly narrated in the Jim–Kenji game (44:39, 48:03, 55:55, 58:38, 1:00:25). These are five events in one game, not five independent samples.
- Greenhouse Propagator: demonstrated mana-plus-synergy role; opponents remove it in both sampled games. That does not by itself establish it as the correct target in every situation.
- Emergency Phytomedic / Seed Suture and Restore with Empathy: concrete synergy lessons, with the support required shown explicitly.
- No Admittance: promising flexible removal with additional selection. Two successful uses in one game are still a tiny sample.
- Kiora of Fire and Ashes and Ajani Unrelenting: examples of threats that generate more than a single body; no general tier inferred.

No defensible “bad cards” list follows from this sample. Jim leaves Medic's Kitesail and Academic Ascent in his published sideboard despite apparent synergies; those are questions for reviewing construction choices, not proof of weakness. Similarly, late Way of the Paradox in the Marshall game is a context-specific recovery attempt, not evidence of general failure.

## Limits and next use

Two games, one produced episode, three players, repeated use of Jim's deck. Kenji reports flooding, and both players discuss unfamiliar card text. The sample is selected for inspectability, not representative performance. No win rates, grades, exhaustive draw counts, unseen-hand reconstruction or causal estimates are justified.

The immediately useful output is a set of timestamped Sealed interaction lessons and provisional card observations. Ratings would need much wider independent game coverage. A future extraction should retain source, time, deck, observed action, support cards, result, ambiguity and interpretation separately.
