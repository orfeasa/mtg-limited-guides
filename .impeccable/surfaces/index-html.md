## Scope and mode

`public/index.html` is an Operate surface for set-by-set Limited preparation before a prerelease or draft.

## Audience, job, and task

An MTG player opens the latest set, learns cards from readable local images, trains exact Limited tiers, attempts deterministic three-card pick drills, and browses the full revealed or ranked card file. They can enlarge any indexed card in place and share the selected guide or exact seeded challenge without creating an account or building a deck.

## Content and constraints

The Hobbit uses the complete, attributable 188-card Untapped.gg snapshot captured on 19 August 2026 plus observed card evidence. Its Training quiz asks for the exact tier in four grouped rows—Top picks, Strong, Playable, and Filler—and follows each answer with In-hand WR, Usually gone by, In-hand games, source context, and adjacent ranks. Reality Fracture uses the current Scryfall preview index and local images without invented Limited ratings; Training remains an unrated card reader with no tier quiz, while Pick drill remains visibly locked. Both sets prefer readable local images and fall back to the matching local thumbnail. Per-set browser storage preserves the current card, colour filter, shuffled queue, exact-tier score, revealed answer, and requeued misses; a miss returns after three intervening cards. Challenge links preserve the exact three-card drill. Keyboard, touch, visible focus, responsive behavior, reduced motion, provenance, offline use, and literal state labels are first-class. No account, analytics, cookies, backend, generic stats dashboard, or deck-builder framing.

## Chosen direction

The Set-Specific Field Guide Shelf: one shared preparation shell with three compact page markers—Training, Pick drill, and All cards—and one authored material world per set. The first viewport opens the latest volume directly onto a focused, full-width rating-training workspace with progress inline below the card. Hobbit preserves Bilbo’s Expedition Atlas with forest cloth, warm map leaves, brass and oxblood wayfinding, river details, Alegreya hierarchy, rounded handled construction, exact-tier rows, and a quiet observed-evidence ledger after the answer. Reality Fracture uses an angular Echoverse dossier with ink-indigo binding, cool paper, Barlow Condensed Black hierarchy, violet/cyan/pink electric seams, clipped facets, intentional two-to-four-pixel cuts, and an explicitly unrated Training reader. All cards opens readable images in a focused lightbox without changing views. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, local imagery, common controls, information order, and 1120/860/700 responsive behavior bind both volumes together.
