## Scope and mode

`public/index.html` is an Operate surface for set-by-set Limited preparation before a prerelease or draft.

## Audience, job, and task

An MTG player chooses a set, learns its cards, rehearses rating bands, attempts deterministic three-card pick drills, compares a small hand-built group of plausible pack cards, and browses the full revealed or ranked card file. They can share the selected guide or the exact seeded challenge with friends without creating an account or building a deck.

## Content and constraints

The Hobbit uses the complete, attributable 188-card Untapped.gg snapshot captured on 19 August 2026. Reality Fracture uses the current Scryfall preview index and local images without invented Limited ratings. Ranking-dependent grade answers, Pick drill, and Compare remain visibly locked while a set's rating status is pending; Study and All cards remain useful. Preparation progress persists per set in browser-local storage, comparisons remain session-only, and challenge links preserve the exact three-card drill. Keyboard, touch, visible focus, responsive behavior, reduced motion, provenance, offline use, and literal state labels are first-class. No account, analytics, cookies, backend, generic stats dashboard, or deck-builder framing.

## Chosen direction

The Set-Specific Field Guide Shelf: one shared preparation shell with four compact page markers—Study, Pick drill, Compare, and All cards—and one authored material world per set. The first viewport leads with the selected volume and data maturity in the binding, then opens onto a large study card beside an honest guide panel. Hobbit preserves Bilbo’s Expedition Atlas with forest cloth, warm map leaves, brass and oxblood wayfinding, river details, Alegreya hierarchy, and rounded handled construction. Reality Fracture uses an angular Echoverse dossier with ink-indigo binding, cool paper, Barlow Condensed Black hierarchy, violet/cyan/pink electric seams, clipped facets, and intentional two-to-four-pixel cuts. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, common controls, information order, and 1120/860/700 responsive behavior bind both volumes together. Final review disposition: ship.
